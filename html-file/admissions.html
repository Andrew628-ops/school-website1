Good questions. Clear answers.

A few things you might be wondering.

Can I apply before choosing a programme?

The form requires a programme choice. Explore our programme pages first or contact the admissions team for guidance.

What happens after I submit?

You receive a unique application reference. Our team can review your application and set its status to received, under review, accepted, or declined. You can see the latest status on the application tracking page.

How do I check my application status?

Use your application reference and the same email address you used to apply. Keep both handy when visiting the tracking page.

Where can I get help?

Send the university a message through the contact page. Choose admissions as the subject so the team understands your question.
